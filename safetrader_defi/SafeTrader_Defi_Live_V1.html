🚀 Quick Start Guide

Choose Your Mode

Use the toggle at the top of the page to switch between:

  • Test Mode: Simulates all trades with virtual money. Perfect for learning.
  • Live Mode: Executes real trades using your MetaMask wallet.

Connect Your Wallet (Live Mode Only)

If using Live Mode:

  1. Click "Connect Wallet" in the Dashboard
  2. Approve the connection in MetaMask
  3. Ensure you're on the correct network (e.g., Arbitrum)

Note: Test Mode works without connecting a wallet.

Set Your Budget

Go to Settings → Your Trading Budget:

  • Test Mode: Set virtual ETH (e.g., 0.0033 ETH ≈ $10)
  • Live Mode: Uses your MetaMask ETH balance

Start Trading

Click "Start Trading" and watch the bot work!

💰 How to Move Funds from Coinbase to MetaMask

Install MetaMask

If you haven't already:

  1. Go to metamask.io
  2. Download the browser extension or mobile app
  3. Create a wallet and securely store your seed phrase

Get Your MetaMask Address

Your wallet address is your public key:

  1. Open MetaMask
  2. Click on your account name at the top
  3. Click "Copy to clipboard"
  4. Your address looks like: 0x1234...5678

Send ETH from Coinbase

Transfer ETH to your MetaMask wallet:

  1. Log in to your Coinbase account
  2. Click "Send" or "Withdraw"
  3. Paste your MetaMask address in the "Recipient" field
  4. Select ETH as the currency
  5. Enter the amount (e.g., 0.01 ETH ≈ $30)
  6. Confirm the transaction
⚠️ Important:
  • Start with a small amount for testing
  • Double-check the recipient address
  • Coinbase may charge a small network fee
  • Transfers typically take 5-30 minutes

Verify in MetaMask

Check that your funds arrived:

  1. Open MetaMask
  2. Check your ETH balance
  3. View transaction history to confirm

Switch to Arbitrum Network

For lower fees, use Arbitrum:

  1. In MetaMask, click the network dropdown
  2. Select "Arbitrum One" (or add it if not visible)

Arbitrum Network Details:

Network Name: Arbitrum One

RPC URL: https://arbitrum-mainnet.infura.io/v3/

Chain ID: 42161

Currency Symbol: ETH

Block Explorer: arbiscan.io

Note: You'll need ETH on Arbitrum for gas fees. Bridge ETH from Ethereum mainnet using the Arbitrum Bridge.
